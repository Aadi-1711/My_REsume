The Best Movies According to Me

My Top 3 picks

1. 3 Idiots

3 Idiots is one of the best indian movies which represents the true reality of the college students and also the bond between each other.

2. Chichore

Chichore is a heartwarming film that explores the themes of love, friendship, and self-discovery in a relatable and engaging manner.

3. My Love

My Love is a romantics film where two people from same school meet each other after many years and they recall the old days.They also talks about their previous love and decide to meet them one last time and tell their feelings. this movie is a great example of letting go.
